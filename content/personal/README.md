* People that are always breaking in new books and never finishing single one of them are considered committing "_杀书头_", beheading a book, one of the ten sins of reading. [And yet.](overview.md)
* There's a proverb from _Analects of Confucius, "三人行，必有我师"_, meaning "_when three people walk together, there is always one I can learn from_.", while i just take it as "[_always stay on the look out for the good traits of others and copy them_](#user-content-fn-1)[^1]". For someone with little original thoughts like me, this proverb is life saving. [This ](my-mentors.md)is a collection of such copy-and-paste efforts.
* In romance novels, they say "_you complete me_". It actually makes sense, I've realized. Only that it's not completion in the sense that the other person is the missing piece of a jigsaw puzzle. It's more like, I've only discovered several new continents of myself after [she ](my-master-florence.md)came into my life.
* Every few years, I uproot and drop into a completely different world, butt-naked, late to the party, nary a clue what is going on. A rational explanation is that I am greedy and always envious of a life in "_Brave New World_". These are some [snapshots ](photo-collections/)of those worlds. Now that it's on github, it can truly last longer.
