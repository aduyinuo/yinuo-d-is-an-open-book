# Diversity, Equity, Inclusion, and Belonging Statement

## Guiding view
Excellence in research and education requires environments where people from different backgrounds can safely contribute, grow, and lead.

## Commitments
- reduce hidden barriers in research training and communication
- build resources that are practical for students with uneven preparation paths
- make mentorship and collaboration expectations explicit
- design tools that lower cognitive and social friction rather than increasing it

## Practice areas
- first-generation and immigrant student support
- psychologically safer research communication
- inclusive teaching materials and participation structures
- open sharing of reusable methods and lessons learned
