# Teaching Statement

## Teaching intent
Teach students to think structurally, reason under uncertainty, and build ethically grounded technical systems.

## Pedagogical commitments
- plain-language conceptual clarity before formal complexity
- repeatable problem-solving workflows
- project-based learning with reflective practice
- explicit support for interdisciplinary and first-generation learners

## Course-building direction
- CTF and security education workflows
- human+AI collaboration literacy
- research methods and scientific reasoning habits

## Evidence in this site
- lecture materials and drafts
- outreach resources
- evolving curriculum design notes
