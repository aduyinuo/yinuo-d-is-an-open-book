# Mentor

I have been lucky in my own advisors, and most of what I know about mentoring I learned by being on the receiving end of it.

What I try to offer: a problem that is real rather than made up for the occasion, enough structure that the first month isn't spent guessing, and a standard that doesn't quietly drop because someone is early in their career.

Students I work with come from doctoral programs, master's theses, course projects, and structured summer programs — [CAHSI LREU](https://cahsi.utep.edu/), [REUSE](https://www.cmu.edu/scs/s3d/reuse/), CMU Path to AI. The route in matters less than whether the question is one they can hold onto.

* [2021–2025, CMU](2021-2025-cmu.md)
