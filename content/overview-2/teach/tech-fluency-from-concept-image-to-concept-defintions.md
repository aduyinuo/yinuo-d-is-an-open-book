# Tech Fluency: from Concept Image to Concept Defintions

_**Three levels of fluency**:_

1. _**One-sentence definition**_**.** Produce a technically defensible definition immediately.
2. _**Discrimination**_**.** Say why this is X rather than neighboring Y or Z.
3. _**Operationalization**_**.** Point at a problem and say exactly what corresponds to each piece of the definition.

_**Project dictionary structure:**_

1. _**Active**_ — must define and use fluently.
2. _**Working**_ — must recognize precisely; lookup is fine.
3. _**Peripheral**_ — know approximately where it belongs and retrieve when necessary.

_**Exemplary working lexicon:**_

> **Stackelberg equilibrium**\
> **Definition:** Leader commits; follower observes and best-responds.\
> **Distinguishes it from:** Nash — simultaneous strategic choice, no commitment advantage.\
> **Operational test:** Can one player commit before the other's decision?\
> **In my project:** Defender commits to a strategy; attacker responds.
