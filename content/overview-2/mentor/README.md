# Mentor

I have been fortunate to receive the best mentorship throughout my educational and research experience. I wish their brilliance and discipline as a researcher and as a person have rubbed off on me. I aspire to pass on the lessons I learned, both from my mentors and my trial and error, to pave the way toward a better future for the young students who deigned to seek my mentorship.

As a female, a person of color, and a first-generation college student in STEM, I am incredibly fortunate to find support from communities such as [WiCyS](https://www.wicys.org/) and [OurCS](https://www.cs.cmu.edu/community-engagement/ourcs/). I am always excited for opportunities to give back.

Students I work with come from doctoral programs, master's theses, course projects, and structured mentorship programs — [CAHSI LREU](https://cahsi.utep.edu/), [REUSE](https://www.cmu.edu/scs/s3d/reuse/), CMU Path to AI.

* [2021–2025, CMU](2021-2025-cmu.md)
