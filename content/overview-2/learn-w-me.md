# Learn w/ Me

## The register

Post-rigorous. Mathematical education passes through a pre-rigorous stage worked in examples and intuitions, a rigorous stage worked formally, and a post-rigorous stage in which the two are held together and the intuition has been educated by the formalism. Stalling at the second stage, with intuition discarded as unrigorous, is the failure these pages are designed against.

So: why a step is taken, not only that it is. Worked, with the derivation visible.
