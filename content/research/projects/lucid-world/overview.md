# LucidWorld

## Collection role
Collection for world-model-centered research tracks, including dependence learning, robust generation, and model-failure analysis. LucidWorld is a collection of projects; Learn Structure is one flagship subproject within it.

## High-level question
What representations make hidden assumptions, causal chains, and strategic options explicit enough for reliable human-agent collaboration?

## Research lines in this collection
- Sample-efficient FOE-Dreamer (2025-2026)
- Trustworthy, controllable, causal world models for operational network defense (manuscript line)
- Learn Structure (2026) - flagship
- When world-model defender agents fail (2026)
