# 20 — Mental Operations Consensus Line

**Prev:** [10 — Difficulty decomposition](10-difficulty-decomposition.md) | **Next:** [30 — Manuscript and formalization track](30-manuscript-and-formalization.md)

## Working problem
Build a usable consensus language for mental operations in CS problem-solving contexts.

## Why this is hard
- terms are overloaded across disciplines
- informal agreement may not transfer into coding/measurement protocols
- consensus must stay useful for both writing and implementation

## Immediate output target
Document one provisional operations taxonomy with explicit unresolved terms.
