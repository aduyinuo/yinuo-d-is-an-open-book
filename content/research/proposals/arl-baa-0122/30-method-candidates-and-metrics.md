## Working claim
Methods should be framed as **claim / evidence / metric bundles**, not as acronym clusters.

## Candidate method board

| Method thread | Immediate measurable proxy |
|---|---|
| Causal workflow diagnosis | failure-localization accuracy and explanation consistency |
| Capability generation with constrained validation | pass rate on security and functional validation checks |
| Peer and self validation under degraded comms | disagreement-detection precision and adaptation latency |
| Meta-policy for automation-worthiness | utility gain vs resource cost under changing mission constraints |

## Challenge-to-metric mapping

| Challenge | Metric category |
|---|---|
| Failures to find solutions | diagnosis, detection, redirection, augmentation success |
| Inefficient solutions | solution-efficiency improvement over repeated tasks |
| Poor transfer | unseen-configuration performance retention |
| Limited resources | graceful-degradation behavior under constrained compute and bandwidth |
