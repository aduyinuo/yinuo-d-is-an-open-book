## Before we meet

If we are setting up a conversation, a short answer to any of these makes it go further:

* What is the topic, in one or two sentences?
* Is there an artifact, draft, or dataset I should read first?
* What decision or feedback do you need from me?
* Is there a deadline attached?

Meetings are usually scheduled over email. Times are US Mountain Time. My availability is on the [Calendar](calendar.md) page.
