# Mental World Modeling

People are the other half of the system, and they are not optimisers.

This line asks what is actually happening while someone works a hard problem, how people read and classify each other over repeated interaction, and how systematic deviation from rationality shapes what an adversary does. Some of it is cognitive science on its own terms. The rest is what an agent needs in order to model the humans it works with and against.

* [**Problem-Solving**](problem-solving/) — small groups, computing work, and capture-the-flag, and whether observers can agree on what they see.
* [**Opponent (Agent) Modeling**](opponent-agent-modeling/) — iterated interaction, categorisation of partners, and adversaries whose biases are built into the reward.
