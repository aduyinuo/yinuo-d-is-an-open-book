# Lexicon

Two objects follow the colon in the title and they are not one object. Open science is a property of what comes out of the laboratory. The health of the scientists is a property of the people it came out of. Almost all of the current writing about agents in research measures the first and assumes the second arrives with it.

The site says the second one twice. Wellbeing is one of the five dimensions every interaction record is annotated along, and the study question is put in those terms: how agents are used in research work, what that use does to research productivity and to research quality, and what it does over time to the growth and health of a research community. Three questions, and the third is the one nobody has an instrument for. A laboratory can raise its output and lose the people who produced it, and the publication record will look identical either way for about two years.

_Laboratory_ here is the social sense and not the equipment sense. The unit is a research group, and the subjects are graduate students and postdoctoral researchers, in Computer Science first and then across the College of Engineering, with graduate students as both the driving force of the study and its subjects. That is a design commitment rather than a convenience, and it constrains the consent model rather than being constrained by it.

## The question the openings table gives this vista

One row on [Work With Me](../../../home/work-with-me.md) assigns a research question here, and it is not the question the artifact pages answer: how can we democratize the benefits of agentic solutions, do people know how to use these systems, and what does an investigation of the open source tool-server ecosystem look like from a human-AI teaming perspective.

Read the second clause literally. It does not ask whether people have access. It asks whether people who have access know how to use what they have. Those are different questions and only the second one is hard. Access is a licensing problem and a procurement problem, and a university solves it with a purchase order. Knowing how to use the thing is a skill-formation problem, and the answer varies inside one lab, inside one week, between two sessions of the same person.

That is why this vista needs a collector and not a survey. The quantity it is after is not per person. It is per session.

The third clause names the substrate the tools actually arrive on. A research group's agent capability comes from open source tool servers in practice, one at a time, installed by whoever needed it that afternoon. The first large study of that ecosystem looked at nearly nineteen hundred open source servers and found general vulnerabilities in 7.2 percent of them and protocol-specific ones in 5.5 percent, with two thirds carrying code smells. Democratizing access to that is also democratizing exposure to it, and the human-AI teaming perspective the question asks for is the part that decides which of the two a given lab gets.

## Why the interaction has to be the record

Research work is moving into agents faster than the evidence about its effects is accumulating, and the evidence that exists is not reassuring. Self-report is therefore not a usable instrument. The interaction itself has to be the record.

Two results stand behind that. The first is a randomized trial with sixteen experienced open source developers on 246 real issues in repositories they maintain. Before the study they forecast that the tools would cut their completion time by 24 percent. After the study they estimated that the tools had cut it by 20 percent. The measured effect was a 19 percent slowdown. Three numbers about the same quantity, two of them produced by the person who did the work, and the sign is wrong in both.

That trial has a sequel, and the sequel changes how the number can be quoted. The rerun in early 2026 reported estimated speedups of minus 18 percent for the returning developers and minus 4 percent for the new ones, and then said its own estimates are an unreliable signal, because some developers declined to take part rather than work without their tools and a large share of those who did take part held back the tasks they expected the tools to help most on. The productivity figure is fragile and I would not build a five-year line on it. The self-knowledge figure is not fragile in the same way, and that is the one this vista uses. Selection changes who was measured. It does not touch the finding that the people who were measured predicted one sign and got the other, about their own work, in their own repositories, twice.

The second is a skill formation experiment. Fifty-two software engineers learning an unfamiliar asynchronous library, randomized on assistance. The assisted group finished in about the same time and scored lower on the comprehension test afterwards, with the largest drop in debugging. Speed unchanged, understanding down. A third result in that same study settles the design: the authors separate six interaction patterns and find that three of them involve cognitive engagement and preserve the learning outcome even under assistance. Full delegation is the pattern that costs.

So the effect of an agent on a researcher is not a property of the researcher and not a property of the tool. It is a property of the interaction, session by session, and it changes within a person across a day. There is no self-report instrument that recovers that. A person cannot report a pattern they were not aware of adopting, and the productivity result says that even the gross direction of the effect is not available to introspection.

The evidence problem has a second face, and it is the one that connects this vista to open science rather than to wellbeing. A survey of autonomous research agents organizes its whole review around one question, which is not whether agents can do science but how a reviewer would know. Of 24 runnable systems, 83 percent released code and 38 percent released the seeds or execution traces that would let somebody reproduce a run. The missing 45 percent is execution traces, which is the same object as the interaction record arriving from the opposite direction. One vista wants the record because it says something about the people. Open science wants the record because without it a result produced with an agent cannot be audited by anybody who was not in the room. One substrate, two obligations, and they do not compete.

## The instrument

_**Agentic laboratory.**_ A research group whose ordinary work has moved into agents, taken as a unit of study. The object is the group and its practice, not the discovery it produces. Not a self-driving laboratory, which is an instrumented physical facility closing a loop from hypothesis to robotic experiment to result with the human out of the cycle, and which the autonomy literature grades on a scale from scripted automation up to closed-loop discovery. An agentic laboratory in this sense has no such scale, because the human is not being removed from the loop and is the thing being measured. The test: who is the subject of the study? If the answer is the discovery pipeline, it is a self-driving laboratory question.

_**Agentic Lab.**_ The collection-and-analysis layer for this vista. Four pieces: a typed event schema, multi-provider ingestion adapters, a provenance graph with team, task and cognition annotations, and weekly diagnostics for data quality and drift, with a consent gate under all four. Not the studies that run on top of it. The test: would removing this component change what a specific study can conclude, or what every study can conclude? The second answer means the component belongs to the layer.

_**Event schema.**_ The fixed set of typed fields every researcher-agent interaction is written into, whatever tool it came from, so that two records from two providers can be counted together. Not a security event schema, which is what the phrase means everywhere else in my work; those normalize machine telemetry for detection, and this one normalizes human-agent exchanges for study, with hard cases a detection schema never has, such as which participant originated a turn and what the person was trying to do. The test: can a record from one provider and a record from another be placed in the same count without a per-provider correction?

_**Ingestion adapter.**_ A per-provider component that reads whatever a given agent platform exports and writes it into the event schema. Not an adapter in the parameter-efficient fine-tuning sense; nothing is learned here, and its correctness is checked by whether the same interaction captured through two adapters produces the same record. The test: if a provider changes its export format next quarter, how many files change? If the schema moves, the schema was written against a provider rather than against the interaction. Multi-provider from the start is a decision about the population rather than about engineering, because a lab does not use one tool and a study that captures one provider measures the subset of the work that happened to land there.

_**Provenance graph.**_ The linked structure over interaction records, annotated with team, task and cognition, so a result can be traced back through the sessions that produced it. Two established senses collide here and they are not the same thing. In my own field a provenance graph is the causal graph built from system audit logs, processes and files and sockets with edges for the operations between them, and it is what a detector for advanced persistent threats runs over. In open science, provenance is entities and activities and agents, recording what produced a piece of data so its reliability can be assessed. This one is the second. The test: is the graph built from telemetry the participants did not author, or from a record of what they did with intent? That difference is why this one needs a consent gate and the security one does not.

_**Diagnostics.**_ The weekly automated checks on data quality and drift over the collected records. Not diagnosis in the CHART sense, where the object being diagnosed is a team's coordination; here the object is the collection itself. The test: if an adapter started dropping every third tool call on Tuesday, when would anyone find out? A collector with no answer to that has a five-year study with an unknown hole in it. An absent record is a known gap and a degraded record is a false one, which is why a substrate that degrades in silence is worse than not collecting at all.

_**Drift.**_ Change over time in what the collection is capturing, as distinct from change in what the researchers are doing. Not drift in the [Toward Deployment](../../3-year-agenda/toward-deployment/lexicon.md) sense, which is distribution shift that matters to a specific deployed policy; that drift is the world moving away from a model, and this one is the instrument moving away from itself. The two need separating because in a five-year study they produce the same signature, a measure that changes, and only one of them is a finding. The test: would the measured change appear if every participant behaved identically to last year?

## Consent, which is what buys the five years

_**Consent gate.**_ The condition on capture. A response is stored only under an explicit consent protocol, and the version of the protocol is stored with the data. Not a consent checkbox, which is a one-time event at enrollment and cannot say what a given record was collected under after the form has been revised. Nor is it a control dependency in the CHART sense; both are gates and both hold an action until permission exists, but a control dependency governs what an agent may do inside a task, and this governs what the researcher may keep afterwards. The test: pick any stored record at random. Can the exact instrument and the exact terms under which it was collected be reconstructed from what is stored, without consulting anyone's memory?

_**Protocol version.**_ The identifier of the consent instrument in force when a record was captured, stored on the record. Not a protocol in the network sense, which is what the word means in every other file in this set, and not a study protocol in the registration sense, which is the plan for the analysis rather than the terms of the capture. Two records six months apart show the same field with different values: is that a change in the participant or a change in what the question asked? Without the version the two are indistinguishable, and the longer the study runs the more of the data has this problem. This is the field that buys the five years. Everything else in the design could be rebuilt later from stored raw data. This one cannot, because the information it holds stops existing the moment the protocol is revised.

## The five dimensions

_**Teamwork.**_ The coordination structure of the interaction. Not taskwork, the content of what was done. The [Human-AI Complementarity](../../3-year-agenda/human-ai-complementarity/lexicon.md) cluster already splits these, and this dimension is the same split applied to research work instead of to network defense. The test: would the record change if the same actions had been taken by one party alone in the same order?

_**Cognition.**_ The reasoning and problem-solving steps inside the interaction. Not output quality, which is what the steps produced. The test: can the annotation be made from the final artifact alone? If yes, it is a quality annotation wearing a cognition label.

_**Science quality.**_ Whether the work meets the standards it claims. Counted separately from productivity, and the pairing is deliberate: this vista's worry is that the two move in opposite directions under agent use while only the first is visible. The test: take the claim the work makes, ask what would have to be true for it to hold, then ask whether the record shows that check being done.

_**Wellbeing.**_ The human's state through the interaction. Not satisfaction with the tool, which is what a product survey measures and which the productivity result shows is not a proxy for anything, since the developers in that trial were slowed and were pleased. The test: is the measure available from the interaction, or does it require asking afterwards? Both kinds are admissible, and only the first survives the self-report problem. This is half of the vista's title and it is also the axis with the weakest instrumentation, which is not a criticism of the design, because nobody has a good one.

_**Organizational dynamics.**_ How the interaction sits inside larger structures of authority and process. Not teamwork, which is coordination among the participants in the session. This is the structure outside the session that decided what the session was for. An advisor's deadline is not in the room and is in the record. The test: could the participants have changed this by agreeing among themselves?

_**Why all five sit on one record.**_ A coordination breakdown is often a wellbeing signal, and a cognitive shortcut is often an organizational pressure. The consequence for anyone building an analysis on top: a finding on one axis is not evidence for that axis until the others have been looked at on the same records. A drop in cognitive engagement across a lab in March is a skill-formation finding, a workload finding, or a finding about when the qualifying exam is, and the three are told apart by the annotations sitting next to it rather than by argument. This is also why the five cannot be split into five instruments, since five separate collections with their own consent terms and their own sampling cannot be joined on the record afterwards, and the joining is where the content is.

## The words the evidence needs

_**Self-report.**_ A measure of research work produced by asking the researcher about it afterwards. The distinction from an interaction record is usually presented as one of accuracy. The productivity result makes it stronger than that: the self-reported and measured values had opposite signs, so the self-report was not a noisy version of the quantity, it was a different quantity. Ruled out as the primary instrument, and not ruled out as a secondary measure, since the gap between what somebody reports and what the record shows is itself one of the more interesting quantities this substrate can produce.

_**Delegation.**_ The interaction pattern in which a task is handed to an agent without the human engaging with how it was done. Not assisted work in general, which is the category the whole debate is usually conducted in and which the skill formation study shows is too coarse to carry a finding. The test: did the human ask the agent anything that was not a request for output? Follow-up questions, requests for explanation, conceptual questions asked while working independently. The presence of those is the difference between the patterns that cost understanding and the ones that do not. This converts the vista's second question, do people know how to use these tools, into something with an answer: the distribution over patterns, per person, over time.

_**Verification gap.**_ The distance between what an agent-assisted result claims and what a reader can check. Not reproducibility in the usual sense, which is a property of a released artifact; this is a property of a review. The test: hand the paper and everything released with it to a competent stranger. Can they tell whether the result holds, without asking the authors? If the answer depends on a session nobody recorded, that session was the missing artifact.

_**Autonomy level.**_ A graded description of how much of a research process an agent carries without a human decision in the loop, from assistive tool use through autonomy over one bounded stage, through a chain of stages from idea to write-up, up to a closed loop where the agent revises its hypotheses on its own results. Not capability: a level says what the system is permitted and structured to do unsupervised, not what it does well. Peripheral to the measurement and central to the framing, because this vista is not about laboratories at high autonomy levels. It is about laboratories at the low ones, which is where all the people are, and which the autonomy literature treats as the starting point rather than as the subject.

_**Democratization.**_ In this vista's question, the distribution of the benefit of agentic tools rather than the distribution of the tools. Access is settled by a site licence. Distribution of benefit is settled by which interaction pattern a person falls into, which is a skill, which is unevenly held, and which nobody currently teaches. The test: two labs with identical tool budgets, does the measure separate them? A measure that cannot is measuring access. If the benefit concentrates in the people who were already good at using these systems, the tooling widens the distribution it was bought to narrow.

## Where the words disagree

_**Trace means three things across my own files.**_ CHART uses trace for the linked Input-Process-Outcome record of a human-AI team session, whose unit is a session with two parties. CyberAgentFlow uses execution trace for the archived record of a single agent's tool use, whose unit is a run with no second participant. Agentic Lab uses interaction trace for both of these as inputs. The third use is the superset and it is fine as long as the schema does not inherit the ambiguity. It cannot, because a team record has a coordination structure with two parties in it and a single-agent record does not, and any count that pools them is counting two different things. The typed schema is where this gets settled: one record type per unit, with the party structure explicit, and interaction trace kept as the folder name rather than as a field.

_**What is built and what is scoped.**_ The artifact README says the build is under way and lists four things as fixed. The design page opens by saying this is the intended design of a tool still on the waitlist. Both can be true of the same object at different dates, and fixed and shipped are different claims. The narrow version is the one to say out loud anywhere this comes up: the schema, the adapters, the graph and the diagnostics are specified and settled, and the system is not deployed.

## Why five years, and what is still open

The claim is longitudinal by construction. What agent use does to a research community over time is not answerable from a cross-section, and a cohort of graduate students takes four to six years to pass through. The instrument also has to survive its own revisions, since a protocol improved mid-study destroys the comparison it was built for unless the version is stored with every record. And the thing being measured is moving: the [competitive learning vista](../what-makes-it-so-difficult-competitive-cybersecurity-learning-and-workforce-expertise-maintenance/) documents a structural break inside one year on a platform with six years of population-scale data, and if the ability distribution in a field can move that far that fast, a one-year measurement of research practice is a measurement of one point on a slope whose direction is what the question was about.

Three things have to be settled before the first record is worth keeping. Which unit of research work an interaction attaches to, because without a unit the annotation reduces to effort, and the algorithm engineering ontology linked from the vista page gives four candidate units for one field while a College of Engineering is not one field. What a wellbeing measure taken from an interaction actually is, since importing a questionnaire onto that axis reintroduces the instrument the design rejected. And how a graduate student consents to being studied by a project they are running, which is the right arrangement for the science and the hardest one for the consent gate, and the answer is not a longer form.

The last one is the one I would settle first, because it is the only one where getting it wrong is not recoverable later.
