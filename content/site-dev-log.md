# Site Dev Log

## 2026-08 — Activity board

The board at [What is she up to?](home/what-is-she-up-to.md) now reads the work itself rather than counting events. Projects are the subfolders under each research collection, grouped by thread and named after their pages here.

Each project says what changed in a sentence, drawn from the diff — new sections, new citations, new functions, new figures — or from the description typed into Clockify, which wins over the generated one. Logged hours shade a GitHub-style heatmap, one across the top and one inside each project.

Where work happened and no time was logged against it, a window asks what it was and posts the entry to Clockify.
