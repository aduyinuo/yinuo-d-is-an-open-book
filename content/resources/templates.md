# Templates

## Talks

_**A LaTeX beamer theme for research talks:**_ large type, sparse slides, one accent color, and no name or institution on any slide unless you switch it on. Open `main.tex` and start typing — it is a working deck, already set up. The demo deck is the manual: every slide shows a component next to the code that produced it, and each snippet also ships as its own file to copy from. Covers layout, figures, tables, math, code, citations, color, animation, speaker notes and hidden slides — notes and hidden slides each have a compiled and a not-compiled mode. A script converts a finished deck to PowerPoint. Compiles with pdfLaTeX; setup notes for Overleaf and VS Code are in the README. [**Download the template (.zip)**](https://github.com/aduyinuo/yinuo-d-is-an-open-book/raw/main/templates/lucid-beamer-theme.zip) · [preview the demo deck (.pdf)](https://github.com/aduyinuo/yinuo-d-is-an-open-book/raw/main/templates/lucid-beamer-theme/demo.pdf)

## Reviews

_**A fillable skeleton for an author response:**_ one section per cluster, four sentence roles per section, and every piece of working apparatus in a macro the exporter strips, so none of it reaches the submission form. Comments are clustered by root cause rather than split into corrections and discussions, because no venue recognizes that split and there is nothing to copy. It carries the posture table that maps each kind of comment to how it gets answered, the three question gate a commitment passes before it enters a draft, and the cut order for a response that runs long. The first section settles whether venues limit the response deliberately. They do, and they say so on the page. [**Download the template (.pdf)**](https://github.com/aduyinuo/yinuo-d-is-an-open-book/raw/main/templates/research-templates/template_rebuttal.pdf)

_**The fill procedure that goes beside it:**_ step seven of the nine step response procedure, opened out into slot by slot instructions. The word budget per cluster, the refuse to start list of what has to exist before a sentence is written, and the exit tests that say a section is finished. The template says what the shape is. This says what order the slots get filled in, and what to do with a slot that will not fill. [**Download the procedure (.pdf)**](https://github.com/aduyinuo/yinuo-d-is-an-open-book/raw/main/templates/research-templates/template_rebuttal_procedure.pdf)

_**A catalog that holds every reviewer comment open at once:**_ eight columns, one row per comment, in reviewer order. Three of the columns characterize from fixed lists: what the comment challenges, what we do with it, and what answering it would take. One column carries the identifiers of the other comments the same change would answer, so the response clusters are read off the table instead of imposed on it. The last two hold two or more ways of answering, most preferred first, and then the choice, or the word open and the name of whatever settles it. A response written straight from the reviews settles the first comment before the last one has been read. This is the thing that stops that. [**Download the template (.pdf)**](https://github.com/aduyinuo/yinuo-d-is-an-open-book/raw/main/templates/research-templates/template_comment_catalog.pdf)

## Experiments

_**Twelve questions answered before the first run:**_ three on the claim, four on the factors, three on the design, two on the runs and the report. The one that decides the rest asks you to write every belief about interaction as a full sentence in the form the effect of A is different when B is present. A ladder of ablations identifies each component's contribution only when the components are additive, and the sentence that sounds like a reason to skip the fourth cell, that ablating B while keeping A does not make sense, is the reason to run it. A decision rule turns the twelve answers into a design, the questionnaire comes back filled in for the FOE-Dreamer paper, and the last section gives the cheapest version that would still hold. [**Download the template (.pdf)**](https://github.com/aduyinuo/yinuo-d-is-an-open-book/raw/main/templates/research-templates/template_experiment_regime.pdf)

_**A map from claims to baselines:**_ one row per claim, and seven roles a baseline can hold, divided by the question the comparison answers rather than by the family of algorithm. The roles are floor, operational reference, paradigm contrast, recipe ancestor, component ablation, confound control, and ceiling. One baseline per claim, because a comparison no claim needs is not evidence, it is a run you have to defend. The section on model-based against model-free lists what has to be identical between the arms, and the two things that can never be made equal and get stated instead. A three question gate comes before any new arm enters a response or a revision, and what leaves it is a commitment naming a configuration and a table, a decline whose reason is never effort, or a numbered offer. [**Download the template (.pdf)**](https://github.com/aduyinuo/yinuo-d-is-an-open-book/raw/main/templates/research-templates/template_baseline_choice.pdf)

_**One sheet per agent, written in the words the security community already uses:**_ a mapping table from what we say to what they say, with the standard each term is fixed in. Threat actor rather than opponent type, tactics and techniques rather than action set, and security posture set by a risk appetite and made operational by a risk tolerance. Nine fields for an attacker and six for a defender, ordered so the posture is written before the reward rather than being whatever the reward happened to imply. Adversary emulation is a claim about provenance and not about fidelity, so a profile invented for a testbed is not emulation however sharp it is. Then three checks, run on the pair and not on either profile alone. The second one is what a reviewer already asked for: the dimension separating two profiles must not be the dimension the method was built to recover. [**Download the template (.pdf)**](https://github.com/aduyinuo/yinuo-d-is-an-open-book/raw/main/templates/research-templates/template_agent_profile.pdf)

## Projects and pages

_**One sheet for designing a project:**_ fifteen fields in an order where each one constrains the next, and a field that cannot be written is the finding rather than a blank. Three gates come first, and they are the condition for the work happening at all rather than a standard of rigor: the infrastructure has to be running and not merely designed, the first evidence for the hypothesis has to be in hand, and the algorithm sketch has to be concrete enough to hand to someone else. Then four tests that say whether to start, a section on slicing one project's claims across the venues whose reviewers can judge them, and a field for what would kill it, written before the work starts. [**Download the template (.pdf)**](https://github.com/aduyinuo/yinuo-d-is-an-open-book/raw/main/templates/research-templates/template_project_design.pdf)

_**Three fields, openable in four minutes:**_ a description, a title, and the one sentence that made you want the page, written down in the shape it arrived in. The third is the field a stub usually skips, and skipping it is why a week later the title no longer says what you meant. The rest is the page shape read off the eight finished blogs on this site, a fill order that puts the verdict line before the body so the body has something to reach, and three skeletons for the three kinds of page. A stub may stay a stub for as long as you like. It may not stay without a description. [**Download the template (.pdf)**](https://github.com/aduyinuo/yinuo-d-is-an-open-book/raw/main/templates/research-templates/template_blog_stub.pdf)

_**One project, three tiers, four fields:**_ the census first, every term the project's own files use, with the sentence that uses it and a locator, no definitions and no selection at that stage. Then the tier, which is what decides how much of an entry gets written, and only the Active tier reaches the website page. Then the four fields, definition, distinguishes it from, operational test, and in my project. Two checks catch a wrong entry rather than a missing one, and any entry whose two locators disagree becomes a clash box, which is what a reviewer trips on and most of what the lexicon is for. The markdown for each project's lexicon page is at the end of the file. [**Download the template (.pdf)**](https://github.com/aduyinuo/yinuo-d-is-an-open-book/raw/main/templates/research-templates/template_lexicon.pdf)

The nine above share one preamble and print as a single document. [**Download the set (.pdf)**](https://github.com/aduyinuo/yinuo-d-is-an-open-book/raw/main/templates/research-templates.pdf)
