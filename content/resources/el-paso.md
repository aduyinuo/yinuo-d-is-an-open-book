# El Paso

Local and practical. Things that took me a while to find out, kept here so the next person spends less time on it.

## Adult ADHD and autism assessment

Provider information for El Paso and Ciudad Juárez is genuinely hard to assemble: insurance directories are out of date, waiting lists are long and unpublished, and the border adds a second set of options that most local listings ignore.

I keep a working list — who assesses adults rather than only children, which practices take which insurance, roughly what the wait looks like, and which options are across the bridge. It changes often enough that publishing a snapshot would do more harm than good. [The current list is here](../.gitbook/assets/adhd-asd-assessment-providers-el-paso-juarez.pdf), and [ask me](../home/contact.md) if you want the parts that are too volatile to publish.
