# Mental

## Dialectical behavior therapy skills

The DBT skills training handouts and worksheets — Marsha Linehan's — are the most practical material I've come across. Developed clinically, taught as skills with exercises rather than as insight, and organized into four modules:

**Mindfulness** — the wise-mind idea, and the observe / describe / participate split. Noticing what is happening before reacting to it.

**Distress tolerance** — what to do in the moment when the situation cannot be fixed right now: getting through a crisis without making it worse, and radical acceptance for the things that are simply true.

**Emotion regulation** — naming the emotion accurately, checking whether it fits the facts, acting opposite to the urge when it doesn't, and reducing the vulnerability that makes everything land harder.

**Interpersonal effectiveness** — asking for what you need and declining what you don't, with attention to the relationship and to your own self-respect rather than only to the outcome.

Two honest caveats. This material was written for use alongside a therapist, not instead of one. And the skills are practice, not reading — the worksheets are the actual content.

## Getting assessed

Finding an adult ADHD or autism assessment provider on this border is harder than it should be, and the useful information is scattered across insurance directories and waiting lists rather than published anywhere. See [El Paso](el-paso.md).

If you are in crisis, please contact a local emergency service or a crisis line rather than a reading list.
