# Mental

**Dialectical behavior therapy skills.** The DBT skills training handouts and worksheets — Marsha Linehan's — are the most practical material I've come across: mindfulness, distress tolerance, emotion regulation, interpersonal effectiveness, taught as skills with exercises rather than as insight. They were developed clinically, and they are not a substitute for working with someone, but the worksheets stand on their own as things to practise.

**Assessment in El Paso / Ciudad Juárez.** Finding an adult ADHD or autism assessment provider on the border is harder than it should be, and the useful information is scattered across insurance directories and waiting lists. I keep a working list; if you're looking, [ask me](../home/contact.md) and I'll share what I have.

If you are in crisis, please contact a local emergency service or a crisis line rather than a reading list.
