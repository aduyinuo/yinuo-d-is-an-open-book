# Respond

An author response is written in a few days, against material that is unpleasant to read, inside a word limit somebody else set. That is the worst available condition under which to work out what a response is for, which is the argument for settling it in advance.

Four things decide the draft: what the evidence says a response can do, what the venue permits it to contain, who is actually reading it, and in what order the work happens.

* [What Moves a Score](what-moves-a-score.md): the measured effect of a response, and the features that predict movement
* [Venues](venues.md): what security venues ask for, and where machine learning venues differ
* [Three Reviewers](three-reviewers.md): answering a panel rather than a person
* [Procedure](procedure.md): the order of the work, and the gate every promise passes
* [Drafting](drafting.md): the shape of one answer, the titles, and the postures
