# Panel Organization

For ACSAC 2026. The panel track runs on a much earlier schedule than every other track, and that is the thing most likely to be missed.

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Abstract deadline | July 8 |
| Notification | July 15 |
| Complete panel details | August 20 |
| Final materials | September 1 |
| Length | No longer than one page |

Posters close September 19 and case studies September 22. Panels close ten weeks before either. A panel is a decision made in spring.

_**What the proposal contains.**_ A title, the moderator, the potential panelists, and "a brief one or two paragraph description." Submitted by email to the panels address with the moderator's name and address.

_**What the chair is selecting for.**_ Panels should "encourage audience participation and focus on the sharp edges of a new and emerging topic where there is controversy or where there are widely varying positions."

That sentence is the criterion. A panel proposed around a topic where the panelists agree is proposing a tutorial. The proposal has to name the disagreement and say who holds which position.

_**What is due later.**_ By September 1: a final description, the panelists and their affiliations, and one paragraph statements from each panelist.

**Verification.** Read once, from the venue's own call, on 26 August 2026. Open it again before relying on a date.
