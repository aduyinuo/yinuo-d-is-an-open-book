# Poster & Work in Progress

For ACSAC 2026, 7 to 11 December, Los Angeles. Venue policies are scoped to a year and this page is not valid for another one.

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Deadline | September 19, 23:59 Anywhere on Earth |
| Notification | Rolling |
| Length | At most 2 pages, including references |
| Template | The 2-column IEEE template |
| Anonymous | **No.** Include name, affiliation, and country |
| Publication surcharge | Not charged for accepted posters |

_**What it is for.**_ The session "provides an opportunity for researchers and practitioners to present their new and innovative preliminary results and latest works-in-progress in an informal, interactive setting." The stated benefit to the presenter is feedback at an early stage rather than a publication.

_**The obligation.**_ "If your proposal is accepted, a presenter is required to register, pay, and attend the conference." You set up beforehand and stand with the poster for about 75 minutes.

_**What two pages implies.**_ Two pages including references is not a short paper. It holds the objective, what has been done, and what comes next. Nothing else fits.

The [Better Poster structure](../../present.md) applies to the board, not to this proposal.
