# Case Study

For ACSAC 2026.

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Deadline | September 22 |
| Notification | October 6 |
| Submitted | A short paragraph, not a paper |
| Published | Not in the proceedings; posted to the ACSAC site afterward |
| Award | ACSAC Best Case Study Award |

_**What the track is.**_ "The Case Studies in Applied Security Track is a critical part of the technical conference. It is an opportunity for professionals to share information that is current without writing a detailed technical paper." Open to "vendors, network providers, systems integrators, government civil/federal/military programs or users across the spectrum of computer security applications."

_**What you send.**_ A short paragraph describing the proposed presentation, indicating how it is important and relevant to today's problems in computer security, plus the presenter's name and affiliation, address, phone number, short biography, title, and abstract.

_**What a good one looks like, in the chairs' words.**_ Describes an application of security concepts or technologies used in a new way. Describes a security architecture. Displays a clear understanding of the relevance of this presentation to related efforts and products, including references as appropriate. Is delivered without accompanying written material. Is timely.

_**What to avoid.**_ "Presentations that could be used essentially unchanged as a sales or marketing pitches." And presentations already delivered in a public forum, as a conference paper, magazine article, journal article, or book chapter.

_**Publication and release.**_ Presentations are posted to the site after the conference. You sign a release, and materials must not be marked restricted, proprietary, or company confidential.

_**Obligation.**_ Presenters are required to register, pay, and attend. Students get reduced registration; nobody else is exempt, including organizers.

_**The official fallback route.**_ The Paper Hints page states it directly: papers not appropriate for the refereed track "may be appropriate for the case studies track," and the other alternative named is a panel proposal.

**Verification.** Read in full from a local capture of the venue's own call. The capture date is not recorded, so open the page again before relying on a date.
