# Career

Advice about academic careers ages badly when it's about tactics and well when it's about disposition. These are the ones that held up.

**[Career advice](https://terrytao.wordpress.com/career-advice/) — Terence Tao.** A collection arranged by career stage. It is unusually free of mystique: work hard, talk to your advisor, make your work available, don't confuse being clever with being useful. Written for mathematicians, but almost none of it is field-specific.

**[Ten Lessons I Wish I Had Been Taught](https://www.ams.org/notices/199701/comm-rota.pdf) — Gian-Carlo Rota.** Four pages, published in the Notices of the AMS in 1997. Includes the advice about giving talks that most people ignore and everyone in the audience wishes they hadn't.

**Advice for graduate students in economics — William Thomson.** Written for economists; the parts about choosing problems, and about what a supervisor is and isn't for, transfer directly.
