# Omni Message Hub Mockup

## Mission
Unify fragmented communication channels into person-centered threads and reduce emotional friction in message handling.

## MVP blocks
- unified inbox by person/thread
- source channel badge and jump link
- read/unread and follow-up states
- draft and dispatch panel

## Relationship signals
- response lag
- open commitments
- pending asks by counterpart
- contact neglect risk indicator

## Constraints
- preserve source-of-truth provenance
- never silently fail delivery
- explicit channel capability map
