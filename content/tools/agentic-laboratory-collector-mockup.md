# Agentic Laboratory Collector Mockup

## Mission
Collect consented researcher-agent interaction traces and support analysis across science quality, teaming, cognition, and wellbeing.

## Data layers
1. interaction events
2. task/team provenance
3. cognitive marker annotations
4. organizational context tags

## Analysis outputs
- graph snapshots
- weekly drift and quality diagnostics
- workload and dependency signals
- method-level evidence of where cognition bottlenecks appear

## Ethics and governance
- explicit consent gates
- role-based visibility
- export and deletion controls
