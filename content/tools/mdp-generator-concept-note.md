# MDP Generator Concept Note (tentative)

## Intent
Generate abstract MDP families with pre-specified structural and domain configurations for controlled experimentation.

## Why this matters
- supports hypothesis-driven method testing
- exposes learnability limits under controlled structural variation
- doubles as both research artifact and reusable tool

## Proposed controls
- state and object composition
- transition-structure class
- reward and adversarial coupling controls
- observability settings
- strategy-space shaping constraints
